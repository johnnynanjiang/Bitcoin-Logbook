The idea

Three simple rules

No charts to read and no screens to watch all day. If you can follow a recipe, you can follow this.

Anyone can do it

Start with $100 or $1,000,000. The rules are the same and the results scale with your amount.

Price drops are sales

Buy in small, regular steps (dollar-cost averaging). When Bitcoin falls, your next step buys more of it. No leverage, so a dip can never wipe you out.

Take profit along the way

When the price jumps, sell a small slice and pocket real cash. Less risk, steadier gains, and you sleep better.
