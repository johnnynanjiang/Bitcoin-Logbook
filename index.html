02 / THE APPROACH

Simple rules. Real discipline.

No complex trading system. Just a repeatable way of thinking about buying, holding and taking profits — without borrowing money.

STEP 01

Start with DCA

Build a position gradually, instead of betting everything on one entry price.

STEP 02

Turn dips into decisions

Reserve cash to buy more when prices fall — according to a budget, not emotion.

STEP 03

Take profits in stages

Sell a portion when your plan calls for it, while keeping the remaining position exposed to BTC.

Your plan, your budget. $100 or $1M.The idea is scalable in principle, but trade sizes, fees, taxes, liquidity and personal risk tolerance matter. No strategy eliminates losses.
