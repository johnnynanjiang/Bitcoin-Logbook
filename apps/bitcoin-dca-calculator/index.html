What is Bitcoin DCA?

Bitcoin DCA (dollar cost averaging) means buying a fixed dollar amount of Bitcoin on a fixed schedule, whatever the price is doing. $100 every Monday. $500 on the 1st of every month. When the price is low, the same $100 buys more BTC. When it is high, it buys less. Over time you pay an average price instead of betting everything on one day.

The real benefit is the behaviour. A schedule decides when you buy, not your mood on a red day. It does not guarantee a profit: DCA into an asset that keeps falling is still a loss, and in a market that mostly rises, a lump sum on day one usually ends up ahead. The longer version, with my own trades as the example, is in What is Bitcoin DCA?

How to use this Bitcoin DCA calculator.

  1. Pick a strategy. Standard DCA buys on a schedule. Lump sum buys once, on the start date.
  2. Set the amount per purchase and how often to buy: daily, weekly, fortnightly or monthly.
  3. Choose a start and end date, anywhere from 18 July 2010 to the last finished UTC day.
  4. Add a fee as a percentage of each purchase, if your exchange charges one.
  5. Click Calculate. Each run is added to Previous results, so you can compare amounts, schedules and dates side by side.

The page opens with one example already calculated: $100 a week from 1 August 2025 to 1 August 2026. That is 53 buys and $5,300 invested. It ends with 0.06466960 BTC, worth $4,058.14 at the 1 August 2026 close of $62,751.88. A −23.43% return. Not a good year to start.

A lump sum of the same $5,300 on 1 August 2025 did worse: $2,935.90, or −44.61%. In a falling year, DCA lost less. That is what it is for.

Bitcoin DCA fees: why small, frequent buys cost more.

A percentage fee costs the same share whether you buy daily or monthly. A flat fee does not. The smaller the purchase, the bigger the bite.

The calculator takes the fee as a percentage. To model a flat fee, divide it by the amount per purchase: $0.99 on a $23 buy is 0.99 ÷ 23 = 4.30%. Check the spread too. Many “zero-fee” recurring buys charge it in the price instead.

How the Bitcoin DCA calculator works.

  • Each purchase buys (amount − fee) ÷ the BTC/USD close of that UTC day.
  • Standard DCA buys the amount per purchase on a schedule counted from the start date: weekly = every 7 days, fortnightly = every 14 days, monthly = same day each month (the last day for shorter months).
  • Lump sum buys the amount per purchase once, on the start date, and holds.
  • Final value = total BTC × the close on the end date. Invested includes fees.
  • Comparison: to compare with a lump sum, run Lump sum with the same total as the amount and the same dates. Previous results lists both runs side by side.
  • No spread, tax, selling costs or interest on uninvested cash.

Bitcoin DCA calculator FAQ.

Can I see how Bitcoin DCA would have done in the past?

Yes. That is what this calculator does. Pick any start and end date since 18 July 2010. It buys at each day's actual close and values your BTC at the end date's close. The results are past performance, not a prediction.

Is there a Bitcoin DCA calculator in Excel?

You don't need one. Click “Download purchases (CSV)” and open the file in Excel or Google Sheets: one row per buy, with date, BTC price, BTC bought, total BTC, invested and value. To build your own sheet, download btc-usd-daily.csv and use BTC bought = (amount − fee) ÷ close, then add up the column.

Is there a Bitcoin DCA calculator app?

This page works like one. It runs in any phone or desktop browser, with nothing to install and no account. On a phone, use Add to Home Screen to open it like an app. Your previous results stay saved in that browser.

Is this Bitcoin DCA calculator free?

Yes. No sign-up, no paywall, no limit on runs. The calculations run in your browser, on a price file that loads with the page.

Is it better to buy Bitcoin daily or weekly?

Without fees, it barely matters. About $100 a month from 1 January 2021 to 15 November 2026 returned +51.05% bought daily, +51.24% weekly and +51.24% monthly. From 1 January 2018: +298.93%, +299.15% and +297.99%. With a flat fee per buy, fewer and larger buys win clearly (see the fee table). Pick the schedule you can keep up.

Does the Bitcoin DCA calculator include fees?

Yes, as a percentage taken out of each purchase. To model a flat fee, divide it by the amount per purchase. It does not include spreads, withdrawal fees, tax or interest on cash you have not invested yet.

Is DCA better than a lump sum for Bitcoin?

Not always. A lump sum beat $100-a-month DCA from 2015, 2018 and 2021, because Bitcoin rose. From August 2025 to August 2026, DCA lost less. To check your own dates, run Standard DCA, then Lump sum with the same total; Previous results shows both side by side. For four strategies tested on the same budget, read Bitcoin DCA vs lump sum.

Why are my numbers different from another Bitcoin DCA calculator?

Usually the price data or the buying rules. This calculator uses Coin Metrics' daily UTC close, values at the end date's close rather than today's price, and counts weekly and monthly buys from your start date. Another tool may use a different exchange, a different time of day, or always buy on a Monday or the 1st.
